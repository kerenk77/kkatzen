Project Details

Book Information

Title: Authors Share Faith

Assignment: Interior illustrations and cover design for a completed manuscript.

Challenge: Creating original contemporary imagery while maintaining a distinctive artistic style that reflects real-life stories of faith and personal growth.

Tools: Adobe Photoshop · AI-Assisted Illustration

Cover Design

Creating the Cover

The cover was designed to capture the sense of hope, inspiration, and spiritual connection found throughout the book. A dramatic composition, carefully crafted lighting, and a unique visual perspective work together to create a memorable first impression while reflecting the emotional tone of the stories inside.

Art Direction

Contemporary Illustrations

Unlike historical or fantasy projects, this book required a modern and realistic visual approach. The illustrations were created to accompany contemporary stories of personal challenges, choices, and faith, making them immediately relatable to today's readers.

Particular attention was given to facial expressions, emotion, and everyday details, allowing both younger and adult readers to connect naturally with the characters and situations portrayed throughout the book.

Creative Process

Developing the Visual Language

The illustrations were developed using AI-assisted creative tools to explore composition and lighting before being extensively refined in Adobe Photoshop. Each image underwent careful digital painting, color correction, and detailed retouching to ensure a consistent artistic language across the entire collection, creating a seamless and engaging reading experience.
